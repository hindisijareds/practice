What is IT Skills Olympics?

The IT Skills Olympics 2025 is a celebration of creativity, innovation, and technical excellence among student developers and designers. Hosted by the University of Makati, this event gathers the brightest minds in IT to compete, collaborate, and craft solutions that focus on the human side of technology.

This year's theme, "Securing the Human Centric: Forging a Resilient Digital Future." challenges participants to design and develop with purpose — ensuring that technology empowers people while promoting trust, security, and sustainability in the digital age.
